# Family Member Guide

Welcome to the **Rajawat Family Tree**! This guide is written for all family members. It explains how to access the site, explore your family tree, and help keep our history accurate.

---

## 🧭 Quick Steps

```
[ Step 1: Open Site ] ➔ [ Step 2: Sign In with Google ] ➔ [ Step 3: Wait for Admin Approval ] ➔ [ Step 4: Explore & Suggest ]
```

---

## 1. Opening the Website and Signing In

1. Open the website link shared by your family administrator on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. In the top-right corner of the page, click **Sign In with Google**.
3. Choose your Google account.
4. If this is your first time signing in, you will see a friendly message:  
   *"Awaiting approval from the family admin."*

---

## 2. Waiting for Approval

For our family's safety and privacy:
- The family tree is completely private.
- No stranger or unapproved account can see names, dates, or photos.
- Once you sign in, the family administrator receives your request. Once they confirm who you are, they will approve your account.
- You only need to do this once. Once approved, you will have immediate access whenever you visit.

---

## 3. How to Explore the Family Tree

Once your account is approved, the interactive family tree will appear:

- **Move Around (Pan)**: Click and drag anywhere on the background, or swipe with your finger on a touch screen.
- **Zoom In / Out**: Use your mouse scroll wheel, pinch to zoom on mobile, or use the **+** and **–** buttons on the bottom-left controls.
- **Fit View**: Click the **Fit View** button in the top bar to zoom out and center the entire family on your screen.
- **Search for a Relative**:
  - In the top bar, click the **Find relative...** box.
  - Type any first name, last name, or nickname (e.g., *Kalyan* or *Vikram*).
  - Click on the person's name in the search results, and the tree will smoothly fly directly to their card!
- **View Person Details**: Click on any person's card to open their profile on the right side. You can read their birth year, profession, hometown, and family memories.
- **Tree View vs People List**:
  - Click **Tree View** for the visual family branch canvas.
  - Click **People list** in the top bar to see a simple, easy-to-read table of all relatives with search and sort.

---

## 4. How to Suggest an Edit or Correction

Found a typo in a name, an incorrect date, or want to add a story? You can easily propose a correction:

1. Click on the relative's card in the tree.
2. In the side panel, click **Suggest Edit** (or **Report Error**).
3. Update the information you want to correct (for example, typing the correct birth year or adding a nickname).
4. Add a short note in the message box explaining the change (for example: *"Updated uncle's college degree"*).
5. Click **Submit for Review**.
6. Your suggestion is sent directly to the family admin's review inbox!

---

## 5. How to Propose a New Relative

Have a new baby in the family, or want to add a sibling or spouse who is not yet in the tree?

1. Click on the closest relative in the tree (for example, the parent or spouse).
2. In the side panel, click **Suggest Relative**.
3. Choose the relationship (e.g. *Child*, *Spouse*, or *Sibling*).
4. Fill in their name, year of birth, and any other details you know.
5. Click **Submit for Review**.
6. The family admin will review and approve the addition into the tree.

---

## 6. Checking "My Suggestions"

Want to see if your suggested changes have been approved?

1. Click on the **My Suggestions** tab in the menu.
2. You will see a list of all suggestions you have submitted, along with their status:
   - 🟡 **Pending**: Waiting for the admin to review.
   - 🟢 **Approved**: Accepted and added to the official family tree!
   - 🔴 **Rejected**: If rejected, you can read the admin's note explaining why (for example, if additional verification was needed).

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can people outside our family find our family tree on Google?**  
**A:** No. Search engines cannot index private documents, and the database requires verified Google authentication with manual administrator approval before any family records can be loaded.

**Q: Can other members see my phone number or home address?**  
**A:** No. Private contact details and exact birth dates for living members are restricted strictly to the administrator's private records.

**Q: Why do I have to wait 60 seconds before submitting another suggestion?**  
**A:** This is a safety measure to protect our free database quota and prevent accidental double submissions.
